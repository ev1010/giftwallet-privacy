Gift Wallet – Privacy Policy

ViseVibe Studio · Last updated: November 14, 2026

לגרסה בעברית

Gift Wallet (“the app”) is published by ViseVibe Studio. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app uses

  • Card details you enter. Names, issuers, amounts and balances, expiry dates, card numbers, PIN codes, links, notes and usage history. They are stored only on your device, inside the app’s private storage.
  • Photos of vouchers. If you add a photo, it is compressed and saved in the app’s private storage on your device. It is never uploaded or shared.
  • Camera. Used only when you choose to photograph a voucher or scan a barcode. Images are processed on the device.
  • Notifications. Expiry reminders are scheduled on the device itself. No server is involved.

What the app does not do

  • It has no internet permission, so it cannot send any information. It has no servers and no accounts.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking functionality.
  • It does not collect, sell or share any personal information.

Keeping your data safe

Card numbers and PIN codes you save are stored on your device. We recommend protecting your device with a screen lock.

Backups

If backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may back up the app’s data (your cards and their history, without photos) to your own Google account, and may copy it, with photos, when you move to a new device. This is done by Android under Google’s privacy policy; the developer has no access to it.

You can also save a backup file, including photos, to a place you choose, such as a folder on your device or your Google Drive. The file contains your card details, so keep it somewhere safe. Once saved or shared, the file is under your control.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

במה האפליקציה משתמשת

  • צילומי שוברים. תמונה שמוסיפים נדחסת ונשמרת באחסון הפרטי של האפליקציה במכשיר. היא לא מועלית ולא משותפת.
  • מצלמה. משמשת רק כשבוחרים לצלם שובר או לסרוק ברקוד. התמונות מעובדות במכשיר.

מה האפליקציה לא עושה

שמירה על המידע

מספרי כרטיס וקודי PIN שנשמרים באפליקציה נמצאים במכשיר שלך. מומלץ להגן על המכשיר בנעילת מסך.
